nPlyr

A video player that does more

Native macOS & iOS player built on AVFoundation. Capture streams from any site with the built-in sniffer, and run mini-programs.

Key Features

Everything you need to play, capture and organize video on Apple platforms

Native Player

One playback core for macOS and iOS powered by AVFoundation (AVPlayer). Plays HLS/m3u8, MP4, FLV, TS and more — with subtitles, audio tracks, playback speed, Picture-in-Picture and AirPlay.

Learn more

Built-in Sniffer

A Chrome-style multi-tab browser injects a media-detection script into every page and surfaces m3u8/mp4/flv/ts direct links and their headers in one drawer.

Runs hikerView-compatible mini-programs and rules natively — the same rule DSL and fy_bridge_app contract, reimplemented on Apple platforms. Browse the community catalog.

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers for macOS and iOS users

Is nPlyr free?
nPlyr is a paid app on the App Store. A free tier lets you try the core player and a limited number of mini-programs; unlocking removes the mini-program limit. There are no accounts and no telemetry.
Which platforms are supported?
nPlyr ships on macOS 14 Sonoma or later (optimized for Apple Silicon, with Intel support) and on iOS / iPadOS 16 or later. Both targets share the same playback core.
Does it use ffmpeg or any external binary?
No. The playback and remuxing core is built entirely on Apple's AVFoundation (AVPlayer). This keeps the app lightweight, sandboxed-friendly and App-Store compliant.
What video formats are supported?
Because it relies on AVFoundation, nPlyr plays whatever the OS decodes natively — HLS (m3u8), fragmented MP4, MP4, MOV, M4V, and containers AVPlayer can read. For HLS, downloads and remuxing to MP4 are handled in-app.
What are mini-programs and are they safe?
Mini-programs are hikerView-compatible rules that let nPlyr browse and parse third-party video sources through a sandboxed JavaScript engine. They are community-provided. See the Compatibility page for exactly which Java/Rhino behaviors are and are not supported.
Does the sniffer work on every site?
The sniffer captures media requests made by the page (fetch/XHR/video.src/MediaSource). Sites that wrap streams in proprietary players or heavy DRM may not expose a direct link. The Mini-Program runtime is the more reliable path for those sources.
