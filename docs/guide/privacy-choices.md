# Privacy Choices

nPlyr is a local-first application. Because your data stays on your device, we do not collect, sell, or share any personal information.

## Data We Hold Locally

nPlyr stores all data locally on your device:

- Watch history, bookmarks and downloads
- App preferences and theming
- Mini-program variables (in local storage, only from rules you import)
- Debug logs (if enabled)

None of this data is uploaded to any server by the app itself.

## Network Is User-Directed

nPlyr uses the network only when you stream, download, browse or run a mini-program you
chose. There are no cloud AI features and no account, so there is nothing to opt in or out
of — the app simply does not send your data to us.

## Your Rights

Since nPlyr does not collect or store personal data on our servers, there is no personal data for us to access, delete, or transfer. All your data can be managed directly within the app or by removing the app's container folder:

```bash
rm -rf ~/Library/Containers/com.w3cub.clipboard
```

## Do Not Sell

nPlyr does not sell any personal information. We do not use third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services of any kind.
