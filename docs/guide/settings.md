# Settings

nPlyr's settings window is organized into tabs. This page walks through the ones most
people change first.

## Appearance & language

### Themes

nPlyr ships with several built-in themes, each keyed to a signature accent color. Switch
themes and the entire UI — including every open window — updates immediately. You can also
follow the **system appearance** (light / dark) automatically.

### Language

The UI is localized into **25 languages**. Changing the language takes effect instantly
across all open windows and the menu bar — no restart required. (This live switch is
handled by rebuilding each window's view when the language changes.)

## General

- **Browser user-agent** — choose *Auto*, *Mobile*, *Desktop (PC)*, or paste a **custom**
  UA. The UA is used by the [sniffer](/guide/browser) and by playback/parsing when needed.
  A rule may also specify its own UA; the gear setting overrides it only when you choose to
  follow the rule's value.
- **File associations** — let nPlyr open local video and audio files (`.mp4`, `.mkv`,
  `.mov`, `.m4v`, `.webm`, `.flv`, `.mka`, …) when you double-click them in Finder.

## Downloads

- Default save location for downloaded media.
- Whether **progressive playback** is offered.
- Quality default and whether to download subtitles automatically.

## Player

- Default playback speed.
- Gesture preferences (trackpad / touch sensitivity).
- PiP and AirPlay defaults.

## Ad-blocking

Subscribe to Chrome-compatible filter lists. nPlyr filters requests in the browser and
during playback. Ad-blocking and progressive playback are mutually exclusive.

## Mini-programs

Manage the imported mini-programs and rule sources: add, edit, reorder and delete. A
**free tier** caps the number of mini-programs; unlocking removes the cap and is stored as
a one-time purchase. See [Mini-Programs](/guide/miniapps).
